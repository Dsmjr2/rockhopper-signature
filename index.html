Rockhopper email signature

Gmail

  1. Click the gear, then See all settings.
  2. Under Signature, click Create new and paste.
  3. Scroll down and click Save Changes.

Outlook

  1. Open Settings, then Mail, then Signatures (desktop: File > Options > Mail > Signatures).
  2. Add a new signature and paste.
  3. Set it as the default and save.

iPhone Mail

  1. Open this page in Safari on the iPhone and tap Copy.
  2. Settings > Apps > Mail > Signature: clear it and paste.
  3. Go back; it saves by itself.
